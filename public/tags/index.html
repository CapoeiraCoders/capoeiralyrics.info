Songs Artists Tags

  • #aberrê 9

    Songs that name Mestre Aberrê, an Angola mestre the repertoire often sets beside Pastinha, Waldemar, and Traíra.

  • #afoxe 1

    Afoxé is carnival street music from Bahia with a candomblé ijexá beat. Some capoeira groups bring these songs into demonstrations and the roda.

  • #africa 4

    Songs that remember Africa as origin, ancestry, and return — the root many lyrics name when they talk about where capoeira comes from.

  • #amizade 7

    Songs about friendship, and how rare a straight friend is inside the game.

  • #angola 45

    Capoeira Angola is the older, closer game: played low and slow, with a strong ritual around the berimbau. The ladainha and chula belong to this circle.

  • #areia 3

    Songs of the sand. Some are the work corrido about gathering it (“Vamos apanhar areia”); others put the beach, or a grain of sand, in the title.

  • #axé 21

    Axé is life force and blessing, a word from candomblé that the roda uses when the energy is high.

  • #bahia 61

    Songs that keep Bahia in the title, or come back to the name line after line.

  • #barrao 15

    Songs associated with Mestre Barrão and the Axé Capoeira repertoire.

  • #benguela 33

    Benguela is a mid-tempo toque and game, between Angola and São Bento Grande: grounded, tricky, and less of a fight than the fast games.

  • #berimbau 45

    Songs about the berimbau, the musical bow that leads the roda: its toques, its voice, and the respect due to it.

  • #besouro 32

    Songs about Besouro Mangangá of Santo Amaro, the capoeirista whose fights turned into legend.

  • #bimba 114

    Songs that name Mestre Bimba (Manoel dos Reis Machado), the teacher who shaped Capoeira Regional in Salvador.

  • #boi 12

    Songs of the ox: the vaqueiro, the bumba meu boi, and the animal that does not come back from the woods.

  • #caiçara 5

    Songs that name Mestre Caiçara.

  • #canjiquinha 8

    Songs that name Mestre Canjiquinha, a singer and Angola mestre from Bahia.

  • #capoeira 34

    Songs that speak of capoeira itself — what it is, who plays, and why it matters — rather than a side story in the lyric.

  • #cavalaria 3

    Cavalaria is a warning toque. It once told the roda that police or outsiders were coming, so the game could change or stop.

  • #chula 1

    In Capoeira Angola, the chula is the praise song after the ladainha. Players stay at the foot of the berimbau; the game begins when it finishes.

  • #cobra 17

    Songs in which the cobra is a figure — the bite, the hole, the coral snake — rather than a passing comparison.

  • #corrido 285

    Corridos are call-and-response songs sung while the game is on. The soloist calls a line and the chorus answers, keeping the roda moving.

  • #culture 2

    Songs about custom and everyday Bahia around the roda: food, feast days, and the life beside the game.

  • #dendê 28

    Dendê is palm oil. In the roda the word means the game has flavor and force.

  • #dinheiro 17

    Songs about money — who has it, who lost it, and what it cannot buy.

  • #escravidão 71

    Songs of slavery: the senzala, the overseer, the whip, and what stayed after abolition.

  • #folklore 52

    Folk themes that sit next to the fight: animals, work, and feast-day stories sung in the round.

  • #ginga 18

    Songs about the ginga, the swaying base step of capoeira, and the malícia inside that movement.

  • #history 82

    Songs that tell capoeira’s past: quilombos, persecution, revolt, and mestres who kept the game alive.

  • #idalina 2

    Traditional corridos that call Idalina — and sometimes Catarina — into the game.

  • #instruments 85

    Songs that name the orchestra of the roda: berimbau, pandeiro, atabaque, agogô, and reco-reco.

  • #iuna 4

    Iúna is a solemn toque reserved for high-graduation games and demonstrations, usually without the usual call-and-response singing.

  • #joão grande 3

    Songs that name Mestre João Grande, the Angola mestre who learned from João Pequeno.

  • #joão pequeno 5

    Songs that name Mestre João Pequeno, the Angola teacher linked with Pastinha.

  • #jogo de dentro 15

    Jogo de dentro is the close game, played where there is no room to run. These songs name it.

  • #ladainha 14

    The ladainha is the opening litany of a Capoeira Angola roda. One voice sings a story or prayer while the circle listens; the game does not start until it ends.

  • #legends 55

    Story songs of the game: Besouro, Saci, and other folk figures whose names still get called in the roda.

  • #liberdade 18

    Songs that speak of liberdade, freedom itself. The Liberdade neighborhood in Salvador, and the epithet in “Waldemar da Liberdade,” are not this tag.

  • #maculele 48

    Maculelê is a stick dance from Bahia, often taught alongside capoeira. These songs belong to that game, not to the roda proper.

  • #malícia 19

    Songs about malícia: showing less than you know, and letting the other player step into the trap.

  • #mandinga 98

    Songs about mandinga: the cunning, almost magical side of capoeira — feints, charm, and not showing everything you know.

  • #mar 83

    Songs of the sea: the tide, the shore, the sailor, and the crossing.

  • #menino 19

    Songs with menino or menina in the title: the boy or the girl the verse is spoken to.

  • #mestres 166

    Songs that name teachers and lineage: who taught whom, and who is owed respect in the roda.

  • #mulher 31

    Songs about women. A title that calls her morena, iaiá, or sinhá is included; a stray “ô iaiá” at the end of a line is not.

  • #nagô 8

    Songs that name nagô, the Yoruba nation in Bahia, as a lineage of capoeira or as a people.

  • #navalha 7

    Songs of the navalha and the sharpened knife, the blade the old game remembers.

  • #orixá 44

    Songs that greet or tell of the orixás of candomblé, whose stories sit under much of the ritual around the roda.

  • #Paraná 5

    Paraná is the answer shouted back in a famous corrido. These are the songs that carry that call in the title, not the state and not Mestre Paraná.

  • #pastinha 49

    Songs that name Mestre Pastinha (Vicente Ferreira Pastinha), the teacher most tied to Capoeira Angola in the twentieth century.

  • #places 150

    Songs that name Bahia, Salvador, the sea, or other places the repertoire keeps coming back to.

  • #quadra 21

    Quadras are short four-line verses. They often sit between the ladainha and the corridos, or stand as compact songs of their own.

  • #quilombo 19

    Songs of the quilombo and of Palmares, the communities built by people who had escaped slavery.

  • #regional 22

    Capoeira Regional is the style created by Mestre Bimba: a faster, more upright game with its own sequence of lessons and songs.

  • #religion 48

    Songs with prayer, saints, orixás, or the church — the spiritual layer of the repertoire.

  • #roda 9

    Songs about the circle itself: who enters, who watches, and how the game is offered at the foot of the berimbau.

  • #saci 4

    Songs of Saci-Pererê, the one-legged trickster of Brazilian folklore.

  • #salvador 9

    Songs that name Salvador, the city of the roda. “São Salvador” is included.

  • #samba de roda 4

    Samba de roda from Recôncavo Baiano, a close cousin of capoeira: clapping, atabaque, and a singer in the round.

  • #Santa Maria 13

    Santa Maria is the toque Mestre Bimba treated as the hymn of Capoeira Regional. These songs name it.

  • #Sao bento grande 26

    São Bento Grande is a fast toque for an open, upright game — the classic Regional rhythm for kicks and escapes.

  • #sao bento pequeno 12

    São Bento Pequeno is a quicker Angola toque, still close to the ground but livelier than the slow Angola game.

  • #saudade 13

    Songs of saudade: longing for a person, a place, or a time that does not come back.

  • #sereia 17

    Songs of the sereia, the mermaid who shows up in stories beside the roda.

  • #suassuna 9

    Songs that name Mestre Suassuna in the lyric. A title that only credits him as the author is not enough.

  • #traíra 14

    Songs that name Mestre Traíra, an old Angola mestre usually remembered with Waldemar and Aberrê.

  • #video 2

    Songs on this site that include a video recording.

  • #volta ao mundo 25

    The volta ao mundo is the walk around the circle that pauses the game and starts it again. These songs name that walk.

  • #waldemar 25

    Songs that name Mestre Waldemar, also sung as Valdemar, the Angola mestre remembered for his roda and his berimbau.

  • #zumbi 16

    Songs that name Zumbi dos Palmares, leader of the great quilombo and a symbol of the fight against slavery.
