Rights

The website code is free to reuse. The song texts are not.

Each lyric, translation, recording, and piece of artwork stays with its author. This site does not own those works and does not grant a license to copy them. Visible songs are published as uncredited repertoire for study of the tradition. Named artists and commercial albums stay off the public pages until a rightsholder allows them.

If you wrote a song that appears here, or you hold the rights in it, you can ask for credit, a correction, or removal. The song will be hidden on the next build after a removal request.
